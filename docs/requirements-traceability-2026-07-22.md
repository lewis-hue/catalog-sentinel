## Non-negotiable interpretation rules

- A synthetic 1,000+ fixture proves application behavior at that size; it does
  not prove that a real Steel lease can extract the same DistroKid catalogue.
- Durable recovery proves reconstruction of work during the original Steel
  lease and immutable scan deadline. It does not renew Steel, preserve a session
  after lease expiry, or eliminate a later DistroKid re-authentication challenge.
- `ABSENT_AT_SOURCE` requires a successfully captured, explicit source-level
  absence. Omission, timeout, parse failure, authorization failure, and unknown
  values are not “missing metadata.”
- Artwork is accepted only from distributor `NETWORK_JSON` evidence. DSP artwork
  and synthesized URLs may not be used to fill a distributor field.
- A saved-snapshot platform recheck is not a distributor refresh. A current
  DistroKid refresh requires consent and a new user-attended Steel session.
- A verified token claim or organization header does not grant shared access.
  Current active database membership and workspace capability must authorize the
  request.
- Source/IaC implementation is not target deployment evidence. Local automation
  cannot waive legal, privacy, security, real-account, Google/DSP, KMS, restore,
  DR/load, or immutable supply-chain acceptance.
