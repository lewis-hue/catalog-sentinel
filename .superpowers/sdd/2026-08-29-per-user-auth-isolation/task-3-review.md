Every re-pointed table's `AlterTable` drops its old scoping column(s) and adds `userId`/`user_id` in the same statement, so no table is left with a dangling FK or a missing `user_id` column. Composite-PK DistroKid children correctly drop+recreate their PK and re-add their composite FK to `DistroKidSnapshotCheckpoint` only after the parent's new PK is created (ordering is safe: FK re-add is in the final block, after all `AlterTable`s).
